Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 22, 2026

1. Who We Are

Saverstore ("we", "us", "our") operates saverstoreofficial.com and related storefronts on Shopify, WooCommerce and Amazon. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you visit or buy from us, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

  • Contact details you give us directly: name, email address, phone number, shipping address.
  • Order information: items purchased, order value, payment status (we never store full card numbers — that's handled by our payment processor).
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device type and pages visited, collected automatically to keep the site working and secure.
  • Communications: anything you send us by email, contact form, or social media.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and ship your orders, and handle returns or refunds.
  • To respond to support requests and answer questions.
  • To send updates you've opted into (such as our newsletter via the Subscribe page).
  • To detect fraud and keep the store secure.
  • To improve the site based on aggregated, anonymized usage trends.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the parties needed to run the business: shipping carriers, payment processors, and the e-commerce platforms (Shopify, WooCommerce, Amazon) we sell through — each bound by their own data protection obligations.

5. Data Retention

We keep order and account information for as long as needed to comply with tax, accounting, and legal obligations, and delete or anonymize it afterward.

6. Your Rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to stop sending you marketing emails, at any time by emailing support@saverstoreofficial.com.

7. Cookies

We use cookies to keep the site functional and to understand how it's used. See our Cookies Policy for details.

8. Children's Privacy

Saverstore is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.

10. Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Reach us at support@saverstoreofficial.com, call +1 (888) 366-2022, or write to us at 13891 Golden Russet Dr, Winter Garden, FL.
