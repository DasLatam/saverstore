Shipping Policy

Home / Shipping Policy

Last updated: June 22, 2026

1. Processing Time

Orders are processed within 1–3 business days of payment confirmation. You'll receive a confirmation email as soon as your order is placed, and a shipping notice once it leaves our warehouse.

2. Shipping Times & Coverage

  • Domestic (US): 4–8 business days after dispatch.
  • International: 7–20 business days after dispatch, depending on destination and customs.

We ship worldwide. Exact delivery windows can vary with carrier load, customs processing, and local holidays.

3. Shipping Costs

Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on destination, weight, and chosen shipping speed. Any applicable promotions (such as free shipping thresholds) will be reflected automatically before you pay.

4. Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number by email. Please allow 24–48 hours for tracking information to update after dispatch.

5. Customs, Duties & Taxes

International orders may be subject to import duties and taxes levied by the destination country. These are the responsibility of the customer and are not included in the order total or shipping cost.

6. Delayed or Missing Orders

If your order is significantly delayed or tracking shows no movement for an extended period, contact us at support@saverstoreofficial.com and we'll investigate with the carrier on your behalf.

7. Incorrect Address

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. If you notice an error, contact us immediately — we can usually correct it before the order ships, but cannot guarantee changes once it's in transit.

8. Contact Us

Questions about a specific order or this policy? Email support@saverstoreofficial.com or call +1 (888) 366-2022.
